## Components

Fable builds things from components: a `CThing` owns a list of `CTC*` components
(350 of them: `CTCHeroStats`, `CTCInventory`, `CTCPhysicsStandard`, ...). Finding the
hero means finding its `CThingPlayerCreature`, then walking its component list.

## Probe (phase 2 tool)

`fable_online_launcher.exe --probe` (or `probe.bat`) makes the mod find
`CThingPlayerCreature` and `CTCHeroStats` in memory and log every float field that
changes, every 2 seconds. Walk around and the position fields show up; take a hit and
the health field shows up. Send the log back and the offsets go into the next note.
